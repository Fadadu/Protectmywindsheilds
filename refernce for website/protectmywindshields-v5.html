The Problem

Most Cars Sit in Heat Longer Than People Realize.

Many cars spend five to eight hours parked in open sunlight every day. Over time, that heat does more than make the car uncomfortable. It slowly affects the interior surfaces and makes cooling harder each time you return.

The steering wheel feels too hot. The air inside feels heavy. The AC takes longer. After months of daily exposure, the effects show on the dashboard and trim.

The Solution

Two Shields. Better Together.

The Exterior Shield sits on the outside of the glass and reflects direct sunlight before it reaches the windshield — stopping heat at the source.

The Interior Shield fits inside the cabin and blocks UV and residual heat from warming the dashboard, seats, and trim throughout the day.

Used together, they create the most effective barrier against daily heat buildup for parked cars.
